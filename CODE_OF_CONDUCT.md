# Code of Conduct

This project is committed to creating a respectful, collaborative, and welcoming environment for everyone.  
All contributors, maintainers, and participants are expected to uphold the standards outlined in this document.

---

## 1. Our Standards

We strive to create a community where people:

- Act with respect and professionalism  
- Communicate clearly and constructively  
- Welcome diverse perspectives and backgrounds  
- Support each other’s learning and growth  
- Collaborate in good faith  

Behaviors that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Offering thoughtful feedback  
- Being open to different viewpoints  
- Taking responsibility for mistakes  
- Showing empathy toward others  

---

## 2. Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not tolerated:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks  
- Threatening or violent language  
- Disruptive or intentionally harmful behavior  
- Sharing private information without consent  
- Any conduct that creates a hostile or unsafe environment  

---

## 3. Responsibilities of Maintainers

Project maintainers are responsible for:

- Clarifying standards of acceptable behavior  
- Reviewing and addressing issues respectfully  
- Taking appropriate action when violations occur  
- Ensuring fair and consistent enforcement  

Maintainers have the right to remove comments, reject contributions, or ban individuals who violate this Code of Conduct.

---

## 4. Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct:

- Contact the project maintainer privately  
- Provide as much detail as possible  
- Reports will be handled confidentially and respectfully  

Maintainers will review the situation and take appropriate action.

---

## 5. Enforcement

Violations may result in:

- Warnings  
- Temporary or permanent removal from the project  
- Reversal of contributions if necessary  

The severity of the action will depend on the nature of the violation.

---

## 6. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- All project spaces (GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions)  
- Direct interactions related to the project  
- Any public space where someone represents the project  

---

## 7. Acknowledgment

This Code of Conduct is inspired by widely adopted open‑source community standards and adapted to fit the goals and values of this project.

By participating in this project, you agree to uphold these guidelines.
